## Limits and governance

Small samples have wide uncertainty; a bug occurring on only 1/3 of seeds is not reliably detected with five trials. MockLLM and public judge replay do not establish real-user performance or a held-out judge result. The eval owner investigates exit 1; update the eval-set version and regenerate the baseline when cases or labels change. Same-version thresholds may only become stricter.
